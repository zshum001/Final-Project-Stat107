Question: How do harmful air pollutant concentrations vary across different months within a year?

Group Members: Crystal Kuong, Gracelynne Mohan, Sumeet Sammi, Zoe Shum

Project Summary:
This study investigates: How do harmful air pollutant concentrations vary across different months of the year? By examining seasonal trends and monthly variations in pollutants such as nitrogen dioxide, carbon monoxide, and other key contributors to air quality, we aim to identify patterns that may arise due to seasonal changes, human activities, or environmental factors. By conducting this study, we were able to look into the variations in pollutant concentrations across different months and the seasonal trends that influence air quality. We observed that nitrogen dioxide levels were consistently high. This is likely due to the combustion of fossil fuels. Pollutants such as ozone and carbon monoxide were observed to have dips during certain seasons. We also observed the inverse relationship between temperature and relative humidity, which related with the seasonal dips.
